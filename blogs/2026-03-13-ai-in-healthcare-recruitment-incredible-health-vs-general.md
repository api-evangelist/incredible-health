Hospitals exploring AI in healthcare recruitment are asking a critical question: Should we adopt a general AI recruiting platform, or choose a healthcare-specific AI solution built for hospital hiring? AI is rapidly transforming talent acquisition and nurse staffing strategies. General-purpose AI recruitment platforms promise faster screening, automated interviews, and reduced recruiter workload.
